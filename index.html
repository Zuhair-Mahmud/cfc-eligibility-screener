1

Status and basis of preparation

This is an independent proof of concept. It was prepared by the author named above and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, commissioned by or operated by the Common Fund for Commodities. It issues no decision on the CFC's behalf.

The screening logic is derived solely from documents the CFC publishes: the Call for Proposals page and its frequently asked questions, the Member States list, the Call for Proposals application form including its supporting document checklist at Section 8.1, and the CFC Exclusion List as annexed to the Sustainability Policy. Those sources are listed at Section 7. No non-public information was used, and no rule has been inferred where the published sources are silent.

The 29th Call for Proposals closed on 1 October 2026 and the 30th Call has been announced as forthcoming. Criteria are therefore stated as published for the most recent closed round, and would require confirmation against the 30th Call once its terms are issued.

Scope

Eligibility screening only. A determination that a proposal meets the published eligibility criteria is not an indication of approval. Commercial viability, impact, credit risk, and social and environmental performance are assessed separately through due diligence and the appraisal of the Consultative Committee and the Executive Board.

2

Purpose

The CFC's published materials indicate deal flow in excess of 400 loan requests a year, with over 500 applications received in a peak year. In 2025, twenty-one projects were approved, representing USD 161.3 million in project value of which USD 21.02 million was financed directly by the CFC. The published calendar for an open call allocates approximately two months to screening by the Secretariat for completeness and basic criteria before proposals are submitted to the Consultative Committee.

A substantial share of that screening resolves against rules that are fixed and published in advance: the location of implementation, the length of the applicant's track record, the fit of the activity with the mandate, the Exclusion List, the size of the request against the applicable ceiling, the co-financing requirement, and the Section 8.1 document set. Each of these can be determined before an applicant assembles a dossier.

This tool applies those rules at the applicant's end. The intended effect is a reduction in the volume of ineligible applications reaching the Secretariat, rather than faster processing of the same volume, together with a clear and immediate answer for applicants who would otherwise wait for a decision they will not receive. Each screening also produces a structured record suitable for the file, set out at Section 6.

4.1

Location of activities

Location

CFC financing is available in respect of its Member States. An organisation registered in a non-member country may apply, provided that all activities financed by the CFC are implemented in a Member State. ELG-01, ELG-02

4.2

Applicant standing

Applicant

Start-up companies are not financed. Eligible proposals must demonstrate a track record of at least three years, addressing substantive as well as financial matters, evidenced by audited financial statements. TRK-01, TRK-02

4.3

Mandate and Exclusion List

Mandate

The purpose of the CFC is to advance development in commodity-dependent developing countries. Matters addressed by other international organisations, including research, humanitarian aid, educational and medical activities, fall outside the mandate. MAN-01, MAN-02

Exclusion List

The CFC maintains an Exclusion List, presented in a format consistent with the IFC Exclusion List, defining activities that do not receive financial support. Items 7 to 11 are applied additionally by financial intermediaries supported by the CFC. A reasonableness test is available where a project would have significant development impact but the circumstances of the country require adjustment. EXC-01

4.4

Financing

Financing

The CFC finances projects through loans and other debt instruments. The published maximum is USD 1.5 million for regular projects and USD 300,000 under the Fast-Track procedure, with flexibility indicated for projects of higher impact supported by a sound case of finance. The amount requested should be matched by at least an equal amount of co-financing. FIN-01, FIN-02, FIN-03

4.5

Supporting documents

Required, Section 8.1

An application form submitted without the documents listed at Section 8.1 is incomplete. DOC-01

Recommended, Section 8.2

Not mandatory at the application stage. Recorded for completeness.

Limitations

    
    
      
  1. Determinations rest entirely on information entered by the applicant and are not verified. In-depth due diligence forms part of the CFC's approval process and is not substituted here.
    2. 
      
  2. Criteria reflect the 29th Call for Proposals as published. The terms of the 30th Call, once issued, would need to be reflected before any operational use.
    3. 
      
  3. The Call for Proposals page states a maximum of USD 1.5 million for regular projects, while the instrument table in the application form shows ranges extending to USD 2,000,000. Requests falling between the two figures are reported as review items rather than failures. Reconciling the two documents would remove an avoidable source of applicant uncertainty.
    4. 
      
  4. The reasonableness test available under the Exclusion List is a matter of judgement reserved to the Secretariat. Where an exclusion is engaged, this tool reports it and does not attempt to apply that test.
    5. 
      
  5. No eligibility rule is encoded where the published sources are silent. Screening criteria applied internally by the Secretariat beyond those published are therefore not represented.
    6. 
      
  6. Operational deployment would require confirmation of every encoded rule by the Secretariat, hosting consistent with the CFC's data protection arrangements, and a defined process for maintaining the rule register as calls are issued.
